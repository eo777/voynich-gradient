Method notes

One joint model of every word spelling, fitted along the proposed bifolium order. Each row is a positional construction effect; each column is one bifolium. The colour shows how the construction's carrier rate changes at that step, separated into gradual movement and signed jumps.

Rates are containing tokens per 10,000 words in each bifolium. Gradual change and signed jump are differences of the fitted gradual and jump rates between consecutive bifolia in path order, so they sum to the combined change. The fit is the 544-effect positional composition model (gradual penalty 0.006, step penalty 0.006); Path 3 is a separate refit of the same kind. Columns are spaced equally by rank; the model itself was fitted along the geometric path coordinate. Overlapping constructions share words, so rows are not independent.
