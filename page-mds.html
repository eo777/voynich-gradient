What defines a page profile?

For each observed construction, count the page’s word tokens carrying it, then divide by the page’s total word count. A token can contribute to several constructions—for example, a prefix, an interior form and a suffix. Each construction is counted at most once per token.

For gradual profiles, take the saved bifolium carrier rates from the normalized joint model with abrupt step coefficients omitted. Expected page count = bifolium rate × page token total. Thus each page receives its token-proportional share of the expected bifolium count and retains the bifolium’s rate. Fractional expected counts are kept. The 50 bifolium profiles are embedded once with equal weight, and their page members share those coordinates; no within-bifolium variation is inferred.

The Jensen–Shannon option compares each construction’s presence and absence probabilities, then combines the 544 comparisons with equal weight. The frequency option compares the 544 rates directly, giving more influence to larger absolute frequency differences. Neither option scales each page to unit vector length or makes the overlapping feature counts sum to one.

The feature inventory was selected by the full gradient fit. The gradual estimates also incorporate its assumed Path 1 chronology, so a smooth progression here is a visualization of that fit, not independent validation. Golf colors do not enter either distance calculation.
