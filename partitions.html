Method notes

A descriptive partition of every spelling into families that never share a word. Starting from all words, each population is split by the construction whose fitted share within it changes most along the order, to depth four. The sixteen leaves sum to 10,000 at every bifolium.

Family volumes include all joint-model effects, but aggregation can hide different movements within a family.

The tree is a descriptive partition selected on the fitted conditional shares weighted by token support, with a 100-token minimum on both branches. It carries no held-out significance and no causal chronology claim. Volumes are per 10,000 words; no token is counted in two families. A family's template strings its positive conditions in the order they occur in its words, merged where adjacent in most tokens, with * for anything else; the rule beneath is the definition, and each card gives the share of the family's tokens the template matches.
