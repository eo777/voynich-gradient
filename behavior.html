Method notes

Group the fitted gradual slopes into shared movement patterns, compare two overlapping constructions through the words they share, and trace any word to the constructions that carry it.

Ward linkage uses the unit-normalized gradual rate slopes of the effects with their own curve. The twelve groups are a descriptive cut, not a count of independent processes.

The overlap figures are predictions of the same joint model, not a separate interaction test.

Group volumes count each carrier word once (the union), so they do not double-count overlaps. Cohort curves sum fitted word probabilities, per 10,000 words. Overlapping constructions share words and are not independent discoveries. Nothing here establishes which change happened first.
