Method notes

The observed carrier rate, the fitted gradual level, the signed jumps, what the fit leaves out, the construction's own log-weight effect and which overlapping effects drive its movement, for any of the 1,324 eligible constructions.

A construction's carrier rate moves through its own fitted effect, through every overlapping effect its words carry, and through normalization against the rest of the vocabulary.

Rates are containing tokens per 10,000 words in each bifolium. Gradual, jump and combined curves are carrier rates of the joint positional word model with and without its step coefficients; they sum exactly. The own coefficient is the construction's centred log-weight term and can be zero while the carrier rate still moves. Nothing on this page is a forecast, and overlapping constructions are not independent.
