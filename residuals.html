Method notes

Observed carrier rate minus the fitted gradual-plus-jumps curve, for every construction and bifolium. Where the residuals concentrate, the fit is missing something: page-to-page variation inside a bifolium, a change the smooth curve cannot follow, or a construction the model only reaches through shared effects.

Residuals are standardized by each construction's binomial scale, so frequent and rare constructions are comparable.

The held-page residual compares each page against the fit that never saw it.

Residual = observed rate − (gradual + jumps), per 10,000 words. Standardized residual divides by max(√(p(1−p))·10,000, 10), where p is the construction's overall share of tokens. The full-fit residual is descriptive. The held-page residual averages, by bifolium, the residuals of pages against page-excluded fits; other pages from the same bifolium still inform those fits, so it is a repeatability check, not a forecast.
